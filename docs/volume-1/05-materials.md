# 5. Material database

`data/materials.gci` is generated by `tools/materials/pnnl15870rev1_to_gci.py`
from the text (`pdftotext -layout`) of PNNL-15870 Rev. 1.

## Conversion rules

- **Composition:** the mass fractions of the table of each material are used.
  Isotopes are summed by element (using the Photon ZA column).
- **Elements with a zero fraction** in the source (Am in Pu compounds) are omitted.
- **Identifiers:** the compendium name becomes CamelCase, and each
  comma-separated part becomes a block joined by `_` (`Tissue, Soft (ICRP)` →
  `Tissue_Soft_ICRP`). The text in parentheses is kept only when needed to tell
  materials apart. `names.json` fixes special cases (`Water, Liquid` → `Water`).
- **Alternative names** become sections with `same_as`. They come from three sources:
  - the index of the compendium ("Perspex (see Lucite)");
  - the abbreviations in parentheses (BGO, LYSO, PMMA);
  - `aliases.json` (NaI, CsI, Quartz…).

  Ambiguous index entries are skipped on purpose.
- **Normalisation:** when the mass fractions of the source do not sum to 1, the
  fact is recorded in the reference of the material, and Guaraci normalises them.
